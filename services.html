Plan Your New Year's Eve in Dubai

New Year's Eve can be a busy period in Dubai. Planning ahead helps you understand your options and prepare for your chosen celebration.

  • Check the event date, boarding time, and departure location.
  • Review prices, inclusions, extra charges, and refund terms.
  • Confirm the guest capacity and any age or dress requirements.
  • Plan transport and allow extra time for holiday traffic.
  • Follow the organizer's latest boarding and safety instructions.
